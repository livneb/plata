Janitor — retention sweeps

What does the janitor do?

Every {{ config_groups[0][1][0].value }} hours the janitor trims Redis streams (heartbeats alone add ~100k entries/day), ages out old knowledge-graph event nodes (keeping ones with measured price impact much longer — they're the historical-analog training set), caps edge evidence lists, and deletes expired rows from Postgres log/archive tables. Entities, lessons, the trade ledger and proposals are never touched by default. Retention windows live in the janitor_config Redis hash — see docs/DATA_RETENTION.md.
